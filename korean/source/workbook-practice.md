# Korean Practice Workbook: Fill in the Blanks

Oct 8, 2026 · @Darren Funes

354 fill-in-the-blank items on the same vocabulary and grammar as the Korean Review Notes, with the answer key in the next tab.

## How to use this workbook

Do one part per session, closed-book, and check it the moment you finish.

| Part | What you do | Items | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Vocabulary in context: write the Korean word from an English cue | 132 | about 20 min |
| B | Grammar forms: put the base word into the right form | 130 | about 25 min |
| C | Mixed review: choose the pattern yourself | 48 | about 10 min |
| D | Fix the mistake: correct the part in \[brackets\] | 34 | about 10 min |
| E | Your turn: finish sentences about your own life | 10 | about 15 min |

1. **Close the notes.** Write in pencil or on a separate sheet so you can redo the workbook later.
2. **Write the full form.** Each blank needs the form that fits the sentence, endings included.
3. **Guess, then mark it.** If you aren't sure, write your best guess and put ? next to it. Count a ? as a miss when you check.
4. **Check right away.** A wrong guess followed straight away by the right answer still helps you learn it (Kornell et al., 2009). Writing the answer and then checking it beats picking from options (Kang et al., 2007).
5. **Follow the note ID.** Every answer names its note (V3, G26). Restudy that note in the Review Notes, then redo the item two or three days later.
6. **Redo whole parts on a schedule.** Redo each part after about a week and again after about three weeks, using the Review tracker in the notes.

Parts A and B are grouped by topic, which is the easier way in. Part C mixes all the grammar, so it feels harder, but mixing look-alike patterns improves long-term retention (Nakata & Suzuki, 2019). Part E has no single answer: write about your own week.

The answer key is its own tab, so print it separately from the questions.

Sources: [Kang, McDermott & Roediger (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1080/09541440601056620) · [Kornell, Hays & Bjork (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0015729) · [Nakata & Suzuki (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1111/modl.12581)
