## How to study

Test yourself before you reread. Every table here has a column you can cover with a sheet of paper while you recall the other one.

1. **Try first.** Before you read a grammar unit, do its self-test cold and guess when unsure. Check the answer right away: a wrong guess followed by the answer beats just reading it (Kornell et al., 2009).
2. **Recall, don't reread.** Cover the 한국어 column, read the English, say the Korean out loud, then check. Self-testing beats rereading on tests days or weeks later (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006).
3. **Mark misses and guesses.** Put a dot next to anything you missed or got right by guessing, and treat guesses as misses. Feedback on shaky correct answers made them stick far better (Butler et al., 2008).
4. **Reach three, and don't drop items early.** In a first session, cycle through the dotted items until each one is right three times. Dropping words after one correct recall cuts later recall (Karpicke & Roediger, 2008; Kornell & Bjork, 2008).
5. **Space the reviews.** Relearn each set or unit about three more times, with growing gaps: roughly 2 days, 1 week, then 3 weeks, then monthly. In each review, go until every item is right once (Rawson & Dunlosky, 2011; Cepeda et al., 2008).
6. **Practice English → Korean.** For a brand-new set, start Korean → English to learn the meanings. From the first review on, go English → Korean, because producing Korean is what class and homework ask for (Webb, 2009).
7. **Keep word sets separate; mix the grammar.** For vocabulary, the limited evidence favors one topic set at a time. For grammar, mixing look-alike patterns improves retention, so use the Mix-ups tab and Part C of the workbook (Nakata & Suzuki, 2019; Brunmair & Richter, 2019).
8. **Make it about you.** The examples come from your own sentences in class. Relating material to yourself tends to improve memory, so add your own news when you practice (Symons & Johnson, 1997).

### Pacing

- One session is 20–30 minutes: reviews due today first, then one new set or unit, then one part of the practice workbook.
- Start one vocabulary set and one grammar unit every 2–3 days. Everything is introduced in about five weeks; after that, sessions are reviews only.
- Workbook misses are labeled with a note ID (V3, G26). Restudy that note, then redo the item two or three days later.

Skip highlighting, recopying and rereading whole pages. They feel productive but show limited benefit (Dunlosky et al., 2013).
