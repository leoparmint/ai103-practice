How this deck works

Everything comes from your Korean Review Notes and Practice Workbook, and the deck refreshes on its own after each lesson update. Study works like Anki with its FSRS scheduler: pick a deck, recall the answer, then grade yourself honestly. Each grade updates an estimate of how well you know that card, and it comes back just as your chance of remembering it drops to your target recall (90% unless you change it in Options). Again brings it back within minutes; Hard, Good and Easy push it further out.

New words start Korean → English; the English → Korean card unlocks once the Korean → English one is scheduled 3 or more days out, so you recognize a word before you have to produce it. Cards you keep forgetting get a Leech tag. Fill-in-the-blank and fix-the-mistake cards check what you type. Practice runs a workbook part or self-test straight through without touching the schedule. Notes has every word and grammar point, searchable. Options can also add a shared Anki deck (.apkg); it is read and kept in this browser only.

No Korean keyboard? Tap 한 next to any answer box for the on-screen Hangul keyboard. On a computer, just type: your keys follow the standard Korean layout (r = ㄱ, k = ㅏ). Shift+Space switches between Korean and English.
